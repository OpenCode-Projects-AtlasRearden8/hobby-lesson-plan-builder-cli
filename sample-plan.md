# AI for Beginners, Week by Week

## Session 1 (~90 minutes)

**Checklist**
- [ ] what is AI (in plain words)
- [ ] why AI is useful for everyday life
- [ ] getting started with a chat assistant
- [ ] asking better questions (prompts)

**Supplies to have ready**
- Notebook and pen
- Computer or laptop with internet
- Something to drink or snack on

## Session 2 (~105 minutes)

**Checklist**

**Supplies to have ready**
